What we're about to set up

Right now your site exists in two places: on your laptop, and in your private GitHub repo. Neither is on the internet.

Cloudflare Pages is a free service that watches your GitHub repo. Every time you git push, it copies the files out of the repo and puts them on Cloudflare's servers around the world. Someone in Kolkata opening your site gets it from a nearby machine — that's why it's fast, and why it can't crash. There's no program running; it's just files being handed out.

The important mental model: Cloudflare serves whatever is in your repo, as-is. You don't "upload" anything. GitHub is the source of truth, and pushing is publishing.
