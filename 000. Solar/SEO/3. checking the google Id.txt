If you don't find it

It's a stale cached copy, not a real problem. In the view-source tab, add ?x=1 to the end of the address and press Enter:

view-source:https://solaremicalculator.pages.dev/?x=1

That forces a genuinely fresh copy. A normal Ctrl+Shift+R doesn't always reach the view-source tab.
