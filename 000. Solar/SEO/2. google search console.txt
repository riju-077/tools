What Search Console is, and why it matters

Right now Google doesn't know your site exists. Nobody links to it, so its crawler has no path to find it. Left alone, that can take months.

Google Search Console is Google's free tool where you say "this site is mine, here's a map of it, please look." It also becomes your feedback loop later — which search terms brought people, which pages Google refuses to index, whether anything's broken. It's the single highest-value thing you can do for the site right now.
