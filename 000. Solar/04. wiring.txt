How it works now (both controls, kept in sync)

There are now two ways to set the down payment, and they mirror each other automatically:

1. Type the loan amount → the slider moves to match, and the down payment updates.
2. Drag the down-payment slider → the loan amount field updates, and the down payment updates.

So someone who wants to pay more than the minimum can just drag the slider up — exactly what you wanted. The slider also won't let them drag below the minimum (10%/20%, or the exact minimum in the in-between cost range), so it can't produce an invalid down payment. The typed loan field can still go below (that's when the helpful orange warning appears with the max-loan suggestion).

Recap of the full input card now:
- KW (typed, decimals) → subsidy only
- Project cost (manual)
- Bank
- Loan amount (typed) + red error if > project cost
- Down payment slider (optional "pay more" control, synced with loan)
- Down payment display (₹ and %)
